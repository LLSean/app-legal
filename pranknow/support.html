Frequently Asked Questions

Why can generation fail sometimes?

Generation depends on network and upstream model availability. If a request fails, retry after a few seconds. If credits were consumed incorrectly, contact support.

How are free credits handled?

Guest users receive limited free credits. Credits are checked on the server per request. Pro users may receive unlimited access depending on active entitlement.

How do I restore subscription access?

Open the Settings page in the app and tap "Restore". The app will request your latest entitlement state from App Store / RevenueCat.

Can I share generated images to social apps?

Yes. Use the sharing actions on the result page. If a specific app cannot open directly, use the system share button as fallback.
